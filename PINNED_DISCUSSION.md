# Looking for feedback from 1000–1600 players

I built Gambit Drill because I kept losing the same Scotch Gambit and Elephant
Gambit positions online, so I made the trainer I wanted. It is free for
personal use, works offline after the first load, and needs no account.

Try the app: **https://cheka-56c.pages.dev/**

If you play around 1000–1600, I’d value feedback on three things:

1. Did the short coach notes explain why a move is played, or state the obvious?
2. Which replies do you meet in the Elephant Gambit that the trainer misses?
3. On your phone, did anything overlap, clip, or shift while you played?

Please avoid posting private game archives, account tokens, or other people's
usernames. Bugs belong in the Bug issue form; line corrections should include
a source so I can check the move.
